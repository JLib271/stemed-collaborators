Search STEMEd+ Commons members by the topics, methods, and skills on their profiles: researchers, K-12 teachers, college instructors, and informal educators. Then visit their profile to get in touch.

Not listed yet? Add yourself

For individuals. Your card comes from your STEMEd+ Commons profile.

  1. New to STEMEd+? Join STEMEd+ first. Sign in through your institution, ORCID, or another provider.
  2. Sign in and open your profile. Choose Edit profile.
  3. Add Academic interests: your topics, plus skills and methods.
    For example: "science identity", "qualitative interviews", "psychometrics", "teacher professional learning".
  4. Add a way to reach you, such as your website, email, or Mastodon, so people who find you can get in touch.
  5. Add the interest so you're listed here.
  6. Save. Your card appears here within a day.

This site is part of STEMEd+ Commons, a Commons within Knowledge Commons. STEMEd+ Commons brings together teachers, researchers, and learning professionals from every field to share work, find each other, and build what comes next. As an open access platform, STEMEd+ Commons helps ensure STEM+ education research and practice is accessible for all people, in all communities. This material is based upon work supported by the National Science Foundation under grants OAC-2226271, DRL-2321946, OAC-2531819 and by Michigan State University through the Jenison Fund.
