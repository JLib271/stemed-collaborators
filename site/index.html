      How it works

      1. Everyone here opted in. People join with the "Join the directory" form, on STEMEd+ Commons or on GitHub. A maintainer checks that the STEMEd+ Commons profile is real before listing them.
      2. Search by what you need and what you offer. "I'm looking for" and "I can offer" use the same list. Each card says why the person matches, and people who offer what you need and are looking for what you offer are marked as a two-way match.
      3. Reach out on STEMEd+ Commons. "Message on STEMEd+ Commons" opens a new private message to the person there (sign in first if you are asked). "Copy a message starter" puts a short, editable introduction on your clipboard.
      4. Profiles stay current. Once a year, everyone gets a private message on STEMEd+ Commons asking whether to stay listed. Reply KEEP and you stay for another year. If there is no reply, a last reminder follows two months later, and the profile is removed three months after the first message. You can rejoin any time.

      About this directory

      STEMEd+ Collaborators is part of STEMEd+ Commons. Listings are public and kept in the directory's GitHub repository. Only what people put in the form is listed. Email addresses are never shown.
