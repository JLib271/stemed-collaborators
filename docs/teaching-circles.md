# Teaching circles on STEMEd+ Commons

Drafts for teaching-circle groups to create on STEMEd+ Commons. After a group exists, add it to
`config/site.json` under `teaching_circles` as `{"name": "...", "url": "..."}` so the Collaborators page
links to it beside "Find a teaching mentor".

Suggested settings for each: **Public** group, forum on, members can invite.

## First-year STEM faculty teaching circle

**Description:** For STEM+ faculty and instructors in their first year or two of teaching their own courses,
at any kind of institution. Bring a class that went sideways, a syllabus you are unsure of, or a question you
did not want to ask in your department. We meet online once a month for an hour, share materials in the group
files, and pair up for friendly, ungraded classroom visits. No experience required; nothing you say leaves the
group.

## Graduate student and postdoc teaching circle

**Description:** For graduate students and postdocs who teach, TA, or want to, in any STEM+ field. Swap
lesson plans, practice a teaching demo before a job interview, draft a teaching statement together, and plan a
small teaching-as-research project. Monthly online meetups plus a shared folder of teaching statements and demo
lessons.

## Two-year college STEM teaching circle

**Description:** For STEM+ faculty at community, technical, and two-year colleges. Share what works with large
teaching loads, open educational resources, dual-enrollment and transfer courses, and labs on a small budget.
Peer observation pairs are matched across colleges so feedback stays friendly and outside your evaluation.

## Discipline circles

Use the same pattern for a single discipline, for example **Chemistry teaching circle** or **Introductory
physics teaching circle**: "For anyone who teaches [discipline] at the college level, from first-year
graduate TAs to department chairs. Share activities and labs, trade classroom visits, and read one teaching
article a month together."

## Running a circle

- Pin a post explaining how to request a peer observer: post in the forum, or find one on
  [STEMEd+ Collaborators](https://jlib271.github.io/stemed-collaborators/?need=Teaching+mentor+or+peer+observer).
- Peer observation works best as a pair visit: meet for 15 minutes beforehand so the teacher names what they
  want feedback on, observe one class, then talk within a week. Feedback goes only to the teacher.
- Link [Where to publish your teaching work](https://jlib271.github.io/STEMEd-Digest/publish.html) and
  [STEMEd+ Faculty Development](https://jlib271.github.io/stemed-faculty-development/) in the group's About page.
